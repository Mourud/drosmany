Conditions treated

What to come in for

Head & neck cancer

  • Oral cavity cancer
  • Thyroid tumours
  • Parotid and salivary gland tumours
  • Neck lumps needing biopsy
  • Reconstruction after cancer surgery

Ear & hearing

  • Ear discharge and chronic infection
  • Perforated eardrum
  • Hearing loss in adults and children
  • Candidacy for a cochlear implant
  • Tinnitus
  • Vertigo and balance disorders

Nose & sinus

  • Allergic rhinitis
  • Chronic and recurrent sinusitis
  • Nasal polyps
  • Deviated nasal septum
  • Nosebleeds
  • Loss of smell

Throat, airway & voice

  • Tonsillitis and adenoid enlargement
  • Hoarseness and voice change
  • Difficulty swallowing
  • Snoring and sleep apnoea

Your visit

How a consultation works

1. Book

Telephone the chamber you’d like to attend. 01330-464290 takes appointments for both, and a serial number can usually be given for the same evening if there’s room.

2. Bring

Previous prescriptions, any earlier audiogram, CT or MRI reports, and a list of the medicines you take. Bring a relative if the patient is a child or elderly.

3. Expect

A history, an examination with the endoscope where needed, and a clear explanation of what is wrong and what the options are. Consultation: ৳1,500 for a new patient, ৳1,000 for a follow-up within one month. Surgical fees are discussed and agreed after consultation, once a procedure is decided on.

Ready to book?

Call either chamber directly, or send a message and they will call you back.
